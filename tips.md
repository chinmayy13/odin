@channel ⚡ Reviewer digest: the calls to keep handy

A quick roundup to help you make consistent calls and keep reviews moving:

🎙️ Narration & pacing
Silence: gaps over 60 seconds fail. A 46–60 second gap gets a growth note.Intro: over 60 seconds fails. A 21–60 second intro can pass with feedback to keep it brief.Typing: fails over 25% of the recording, or over 35% for Coding / Content & Writing. Exactly 25%/35% does not fail.Background noise: obscuring 30–40% of narration is option 2; over 40% fails. Loud typing is fine if narration stays clear.Narration slips: occasional “here/this,” missed cues, or brief stumbles alone don’t fail. Approve with a growth note if the task otherwise passes.
🖥️ Recording setup
Window overlap: 6–30 seconds is option 2. If that’s the only issue, approve with a short growth note.Dock: must be visible at the beginning and end—it doesn’t need to stay visible throughout.Video length: reviewer acceptance is 15:00–54:59, including intro and epilogue. Writer planning guidance remains 15:00–50:59.Missing epilogue: use “Missing epilogue — does not block approval.” Don’t send back solely for a missing epilogue, regardless of idea approval date.
✅ Fix, approve & move forward
Metadata.rtf: below 80% similarity fails the check. Correct metadata yourself and re-upload; don’t send back for metadata alone.Project references: Mercor, Odin or Studio information alone does not block approval. Keep checking independently for PII.Minor on-screen profanity: can pass if the writer didn’t type it. Spoken or writer-typed profanity still fails.Copyright: apply asset-licensing rules to material included in deliverables.zip. Browsing a website with a logo is fine; public-repository exceptions still apply.AI: ordinary incidental enhancements may be acceptable. That exception doesn’t automatically cover substantive AI task tools—escalate unclear cases.
⏱️ Confirmed a critical issue? Spend about 10 more minutes scanning for other critical issues, then send back with clear, timestamped feedback. This shortcut is for reviewer reviews; QC still completes the full review ‼️
