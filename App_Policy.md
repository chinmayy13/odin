# App policy

## Current app policy

Effective 2026-10-16 18:08 PDT. Source checked 2026-10-16. Source guidelines.

Apply this policy to new work from October 16, 2026 at 18:08 PDT. Do not newly restrict work already in flight. Revisit tasks and ideas rejected solely for an app now permitted, preserve the original work, and continue after any remaining quality checks.

Apps not listed here need no separate app approval. Follow the task, account, privacy and licensing requirements. AI use during recording remains prohibited, including AI tools opened in a permitted browser. A preference for alternatives is not a ban.

Project operating rule: minimal use must be less than 20% of the task (strictly <20%). The client guideline names the category; this is the project's threshold. Background-only apps still must not be actively used, and supporting apps must not become the main application.
